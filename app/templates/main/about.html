ASTRA VITAL — Academic Edition (Python Flask + SQLite)

This is a university adaptation of ASTRA VITAL — Intelligent Astronaut Health Monitoring System, the project submitted by team NYXARA NOVA (Trujillo, Peru) to the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 ("Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions"). The NASA submission was the static web edition (etner249.github.io/astra-vital, source github.com/Etner249/astra-vital). This Flask edition was built afterwards for the university course and was not part of the NASA submission.

Team: Etner Therry Rios Vasquez · Jeanfranco Alfredo Ibarra Kocfú · Darlin Saldaña Pinedo · Máximo Fabrizzio Espinoza Peche. We control the unseen.

Educational prototype — not a medical device; it does not diagnose. Demo astronauts and measurements are synthetic. Not an official NASA product. AI assistance (Claude, Anthropic) was used for coding and documentation; the rule engine contains no AI model.
