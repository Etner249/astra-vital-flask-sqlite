Inspiration4 clinical laboratory data

Real pre-flight (L-92, L-44, L-3) and post-flight (R+1, R+45, R+82, R+194) laboratory values from the four civilian crew members of the SpaceX Inspiration4 mission (3 days in orbit, 2021), published in NASA's Open Science Data Repository. These are not continuous in-flight vital signs; with n = 4 no change is attributed to spaceflight. They are shown as research context and to demonstrate comparison with lab reference ranges. Values are unchanged from the source files.

OSD-575

SpaceX Inspiration4 Blood Serum Metabolic Panel and Immune/Cardiac Cytokine Arrays. File used: LSDS-8_Comprehensive_Metabolic_Panel_CMP_TRANSFORMED.csv. osdr.nasa.gov/…/OSD-575

OSD-569

Whole Blood Measurements from the SpaceX Inspiration4 Mission (CBC). File used: LSDS-7_Complete_Blood_Count_CBC_TRANSFORMED.csv. osdr.nasa.gov/…/OSD-569
